# Master Prompt: Dainamo Holdings Company Profile

Create a premium, presentation-ready company profile for **Dainamo Holdings (Pty) Ltd**, a Johannesburg-based specialist contractor for construction, protective finishes, waterproofing, damp proofing, and planned building maintenance.

This is a real company profile for prospective commercial clients, property managers, facilities teams, hospitals, warehouses, shopping centres, residential complexes, developers, and managing agents. It must feel credible, capable, and specific. It must not look like a generic construction template, a handyman flyer, or an AI-generated moodboard.

## Core message

The profile should make one argument:

> Dainamo is the dependable specialist partner for protecting, maintaining, and improving the places businesses rely on.

The tone is assured, practical, technically literate, and human. Lead with evidence and capability rather than exaggerated promises. Make the reader feel that Dainamo understands operational environments and will take responsibility for the work.

## Visual direction

### Overall character

Make the profile feel like a high-end specialist contractor's capability dossier: calm, structured, tactile, and confident. Combine real site evidence with precise editorial typography and measured technical detail.

Use visual rhythm across the deck:

- quiet, spacious opening
- image-led proof
- technical capability
- practical process
- human and commercial reassurance
- strong but restrained close

Do not make every page a photo grid. Alternate between full-bleed photography, generous white/paper space, deep-blue bands, structured service diagrams, and detail crops.

### Colour system

Use these tokens as named Figma colour styles:

- `Dainamo Navy` `#071B4A`
- `Dainamo Blue` `#1259B3`
- `Royal Blue` `#1B5CD0`
- `Safety Gold` `#D69316`
- `Paper` `#F7F8FA`
- `Surface` `#FCFDFE`
- `Ink` `#172033`
- `Muted` `#667085`
- `Line` `#D9DEE8`

Use navy for authority, structure, and dark image overlays. Use blue for active emphasis and primary actions. Use gold sparingly for a key figure, rule, marker, or commercial emphasis. Do not use gold as a decorative background and do not introduce purple gradients, neon accents, or a muddy industrial brown palette.

### Typography

Use `Archivo` for display headings, labels, figures, and compact technical metadata. Use `Manrope` for body copy and longer reading text. If those fonts are unavailable in Figma, use `Geist` as the first substitute and a clean grotesque sans-serif as the second substitute.

Typography should be direct and editorial:

- oversized but controlled cover title
- short headlines with no awkward multi-line wraps
- compact uppercase section labels with generous tracking
- readable body copy with comfortable line height
- strong numeric or service markers

Do not use more than two font families. Do not use decorative, futuristic, condensed, or pseudo-technical typefaces.

### Layout and composition

- Use a disciplined 12-column grid for A4 pages.
- Keep generous outer margins and clear gutters.
- Use sharp geometry with 2-5px corner radii; avoid bubbly SaaS cards.
- Prefer flat surfaces, borders, rules, bands, and tonal paper changes over heavy shadows.
- Use asymmetry selectively: a large image crop, a narrow text column, or an oversized service label can create character without harming scanability.
- Use large image crops that show the material and workmanship. Avoid dark, blurry, overly filtered, or generic atmospheric images.
- Keep captions and labels close to their images and make every image understandable without relying on colour alone.
- Maintain strong contrast and minimum readable body text appropriate for a printed PDF.

### Image treatment

- Preserve the authenticity of the supplied images.
- Do not generate people, logos, signage, uniforms, or project outcomes that are not in the sources.
- Do not overuse rounded masks, fake polaroids, device mockups, or floating glass panels.
- Use one dominant image per page where possible, with supporting details used deliberately.
- Add neutral crop notes in the image library for `hero`, `wide`, `portrait`, and `detail` variants.
- Where a photo contains useful work detail, crop tightly rather than covering it with text.

## Quality bar and final checks

Before finishing, check that:

- The logo is used consistently and has clear space.
- The first two pages communicate what Dainamo does within seconds.
- Real project photography is more prominent than decorative graphics.
- Specialist work is foregrounded; the company does not read as a generic handyman business.
- Every factual statement can be traced to the supplied references.
- No client is presented as an endorsement without explicit evidence.
- No banking details or private operational credentials appear.
- Text is editable and not baked into image exports.
- All page numbers, headings, captions, contact details, and logo variants are aligned consistently.
- The deck remains legible at A4 print size and in a 16:9 presentation view.
- The file contains no placeholder copy, lorem ipsum, unlicensed logos, fake certifications, or unexplained decorative elements.

The final result should feel like Dainamo has earned the right to handle serious sites: visually precise, commercially literate, technically grounded, and built from evidence.
