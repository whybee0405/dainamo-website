# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Facilities and property managers, body corporate trustees, managing agents, procurement teams and
owners of commercial and managed property in Johannesburg and Gauteng: shopping centres, hospitals
and clinics, warehouses, offices, and townhouse complexes. They arrive with a specific problem (a
leaking roof, a failing floor, damp walls, a maintenance budget to spend) and often have to justify
the contractor to a board, a body corporate or an insurer. Many read on a phone between site walks.

The job to be done: decide whether Dainamo can do this specialist work properly and safely without
closing the building, then request a site assessment or call.

## Product Purpose

The marketing site for Dainamo Holdings (Pty) Ltd, a Johannesburg specialist contractor for epoxy and
resin flooring, waterproofing, damp proofing, protective coatings and planned maintenance. It exists
to show real work, explain how jobs are scoped and run, and turn a visit into a site assessment
request, a phone call or a WhatsApp message. Success is qualified enquiries from commercial and
managed property clients.

## Brand Personality

Assured, practical, technically literate. Plain spoken and human, like a competent site manager who
explains what is underneath the finish. Evidence first: real photographs, measured figures, itemised
scopes. Never boastful, never salesy, and never making claims the company has not verified.

## Anti-references

- Generic construction templates: hard hats on white, stock photos of smiling engineers, orange and
  black "we build dreams" layouts.
- Handyman flyers: service lists with clip-art icons, price shouting, starbursts.
- AI moodboards and SaaS landing tropes: gradient text, glass cards, hero metrics, identical icon card
  grids, testimonial carousels, invented statistics or badges.
- Dark cinematic "luxury" sites that hide the actual work.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work. Real Dainamo site photographs carry every claim; generated images only set the
   scene and are labelled as illustrative.
2. Write down the specifics. Measured areas, systems, cure times and scope lines beat adjectives.
3. Respect the reader's time. One clear action per section (request an assessment, call, WhatsApp),
   reachable from a phone in two taps.
4. Quiet interface, loud evidence. The UI stays calm and precise so the photographs and figures lead.
5. Nothing invented. No certifications, guarantees, client logos or numbers that are not confirmed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast measured against the rendered background, 44px touch targets on
phones, visible focus states, full keyboard use of every control (including the before and after
slider and the photo viewer), meaningful alt text, and every animation disabled under
prefers-reduced-motion. `node tests/audit.mjs` enforces most of this against the running site.
